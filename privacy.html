Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WebIntel Tools ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and the WebIntel Uploader application.

WebIntel Uploader (YouTube API application)

WebIntel Uploader is an internal tool that uses the YouTube API Services solely to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel, WebIntel Tools. It is not offered to the public and is not used to access any other user's account or data.

  • Data accessed: With our own authorization through Google OAuth, the app requests permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic information about our own channel and videos (youtube.readonly), such as channel name and video upload status.
  • How data is used: Only to upload our videos, set their titles, descriptions and tags, and confirm that uploads succeeded.
  • Storage: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on our own machine and are never transmitted to any third party or server other than Google's.
  • Sharing: We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose any data obtained through the YouTube API Services to anyone.
  • Revoking access: Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

By using the YouTube API Services, the app is bound by and complies with the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
